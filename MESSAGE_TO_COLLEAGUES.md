# Message to colleagues - ready to send

Fill in the two names, then send. Works as an email or a long WhatsApp message.

---

Hi both,

I've been building DroneHub, a marketplace connecting drone operators with
customers who need aerial work. I'd like you to try it properly before I take it
any further, and I'd rather hear what's wrong now than after I've shown it to
anyone else.

It's a real working build, not a mockup or a clickable prototype. Everything you
do creates real data in the system. It should take about 20 minutes.

**The site:** https://www.dronehub.global

Please sign up with a password you don't use anywhere else, and don't upload any
real licences, insurance documents or anything sensitive. Treat it as a test
environment.

## Who's doing what

**[NAME 1] - you're the OPERATOR**, the drone pilot offering services.
**[NAME 2] - you're the CLIENT**, the customer who needs drone work done.

Please go in order, because each step depends on the one before it. Drop a message
in the group when you've finished your step so the next person knows to go.

---

### Step 1 - [NAME 1]: sign up as an operator

Go to https://www.dronehub.global/register/operator, fill in your details, and pick
the services you'd offer.

**Then you'll be waiting.** Operators have to be approved before they can bid, so
your dashboard will look empty and you won't see any jobs. That's meant to happen,
it's not broken. Message me and I'll approve you within a few minutes, then refresh.

### Step 2 - [NAME 2]: sign up as a client and post a job

Go to https://www.dronehub.global/register/client and create your account.

From your dashboard, click "Post a Job". Give it a title, description, category and
location. Anything realistic is fine, for example "Roof inspection, 12-storey
building in Kwun Tong". Message the group once it's up.

### Step 3 - [NAME 1]: find the job and apply

Once I've approved you, go to "Browse Jobs", find [NAME 2]'s job, open it, and
submit a proposal with your price, delivery time and a short cover note.

### Step 4 - [NAME 2]: review and award

Open your job from your dashboard. You should see the proposal. Read it, then click
"Award This Job". Have a go at messaging the operator from the job page too.

### Step 5 - both: poke around

Look at the operator directory, profiles, your account settings, the notifications
bell. Try to break it.

## What's deliberately not finished yet

So you don't waste time reporting things I already know:

- **No emails are sent.** Notifications only appear inside the site, via the bell
  icon. You will never receive an email, including for approvals or new proposals.
- **Payments are switched off.** You can't pay for anything. The checkout exists but
  has no payment keys connected.
- **Uploaded files may disappear.** File storage isn't permanent yet, so any
  document or photo you upload can vanish when I next update the site. Another
  reason not to upload anything real.
- **Google sign-in is brand new.** It works, but email and password is the
  better-tested route for this test. If you do use Google, it'll ask you to choose
  Client or Operator afterwards.
- **A few admin features show "Coming soon".** That's intentional.

Everything else, your account, the job, proposals, messages and reviews, is stored
properly and will still be there tomorrow.

## What I actually want to know

Please be blunt. Politeness is not useful to me here.

1. **Where did you hesitate?** Any point where you weren't sure what to click, or
   what a word meant, is the most valuable thing you can tell me.
2. **Did anything look broken, wrong or unfinished?**
3. **Did any wording feel odd, jargon-heavy or just not like plain English?**
4. **[NAME 1]:** was it clear what you were signing up for and what you'd get out of
   it as an operator? Would the approval wait annoy you in real life?
5. **[NAME 2]:** was posting a job quick enough? Would you trust this enough to hire
   a stranger through it?
6. **Would you actually use this?** If not, what's missing?

Screenshots are gold, especially of anything confusing. Let me know which browser
you used and whether you were on a phone or a laptop.
